
RTTTL NOTATION — Feluda Theme
Satyajit Ray's signature score (Sonar Kella, 1974)

Notes sourced from a veteran YouTuber who transcribed the theme by ear
and listed them in a video description. Credit to that unnamed careful
person who wrote it down for no reason other than love of the tune.

Note durations and rest periods were not in the source. Those were tuned
by listening to the original score repeatedly until the timing felt right —
not until the math was correct, until it sounded like Feluda.

The firmware does not use this file at runtime. Note frequencies and
durations are hardcoded directly. This notation is preserved for
documentation and for anyone who wants to port the melody elsewhere.

RTTTL (Ring Tone Text Transfer Language) was Nokia's format for composable
ringtones in the late 1990s. You could write a melody in plain text and send
it via SMS. An entire generation composed music this way, mostly while
avoiding homework. It never needed to outlive its era. It did anyway.
